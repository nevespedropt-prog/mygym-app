On the menu

Starters: Prawn and Devon crab tian, venison terrine with fig chutney, French onion soup, smoked salmon and fennel salad, wild mushrooms on toasted sourdough.

Mains: roast turkey with crispy chicken and pancetta terrine and all the trimmings, slow-roasted lamb shoulder, pan-fried trout with garlic prawns, sirloin steak, a double beef and venison burger, or a spiced chickpea, sweet potato and cauliflower pie (vegan).

Puddings: traditional Christmas pudding, chocolate, cherry and amaretto sundae, sticky toffee pudding, apple crumble tart, or the cheese board.

How booking works

The pub takes a non-refundable deposit of £10 per person, and a table isn't confirmed until that deposit is paid. For a group our size they also need everyone's food order in advance.

We'll book as soon as we have a winner, then send you the deposit details and ask for your choices.

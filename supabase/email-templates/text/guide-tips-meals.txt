Eating well does not need hours in the kitchen. Here are four simple ways to make it easier.

- Pick one prep day. Sunday afternoon works for most people. Batch-cook two proteins, like chicken thighs and lentils, with a tray of roasted veg and a pot of rice or potatoes.
- Build every plate around protein. Aim for a palm-sized portion at each meal: chicken, fish, eggs, Greek yoghurt, tofu or beans.
- Keep it simple. Three or four meals you enjoy, repeated through the week, are easier to stick to than a new recipe every night.
- Have snacks ready. Boiled eggs, yoghurt pots, fruit and a handful of nuts stop you grabbing whatever is nearby.

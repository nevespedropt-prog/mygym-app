WARM UP BEFORE YOU TRAIN

Hi {{first_name}},

Here is something you can use today. Spend five to ten minutes warming up before every session. It raises your heart rate, loosens your joints and gets your body ready to work.

- Arm circles: ten forwards and ten backwards. Start small and get bigger.
- Knee raises: ten per leg, lifting each knee to hip height. Hold a wall if you need help with balance.
- Band pull-aparts: ten to fifteen slow reps with a light band at chest height.

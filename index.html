How MY GYM London uses your information (tap to read)

Who we are: MY GYM London, Main Avenue, Bush Hill Park, Enfield EN1 1DS. Contact: info@mygymlondon.co.uk.

What we keep: the health and allergy answers you give on this form. If you tick the optional box, also the weight and body fat measurements you log in the app.

Why: to keep you safe in classes and the gym and to plan your training. We do not use it for marketing and we never sell it.

Why we may do this: you give us your explicit consent. You can say no, and you can withdraw your consent at any time.

Who sees it: only MY GYM London coaches and management who need it. It is stored securely with our database provider.

How long: while you are a member. We delete it if you withdraw consent, if you delete your account, or if you have not used the app for 12 months.

Your rights: you can see, correct or delete your answers, turn weight and body fat tracking off (which deletes your measurements), or withdraw your consent, in Profile → Health & consent or by emailing us. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
